# Grant Davies

UK-based digital lead, trading as GSD Works.

I run ecommerce sites end to end: Shopify development and theme work, replatforms and migrations, catalogue and product data, conversion work, lifecycle email marketing. Then the parts that quietly decide whether any of it holds up. ERP and warehouse systems, product feeds, analytics, email infrastructure. That's usually where I end up, and usually where the real problem was.

Some clients I'm the whole technical function. Others I'm one part of a bigger team. Either way you get a straight answer about what's worth doing and what it'll cost. I've rebuilt stores that had outgrown their platform, sorted out the operational mess underneath them, and stayed on afterwards to help with the decisions that come next.

Same for service businesses selling enquiries and bookings rather than products, and I design and build editorial marketing sites.

Currently taking on new work with selected clients.
